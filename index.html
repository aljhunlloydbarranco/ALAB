Current Work

Research Focus

🦐

IMTA & Aquaculture Systems

Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture simulation combining milkfish and extractive species. Modeling involves Monte Carlo simulation, carrying capacity, NPV and break-even financial analysis, Sobol sensitivity, and XGBoost/RF ML feature importance — referencing DENR DAO 2016-08 and BFAR standards.

IMTA Monte Carlo R / Shiny BFAR Standards
🔬

Phytoplankton Community Dynamics

Bimonthly monitoring of phytoplankton at Tagabuli Bay (Dec 2024–Mar 2026; 81 records, 9 sampling events). Dominant taxa: Chaetoceros, Skeletonema, Thalassionema, and Ceratium, with a bloom event recorded Aug–Oct 2025.

PERMANOVA NMDS Shannon H′ Bloom Dynamics
🌊

Coastal Water Quality Monitoring

Spatiotemporal water quality assessment (DO, salinity, pH, temperature) at Tagabuli Bay using Hanna HI98194 meter data, QGIS interpolation and animated spatial maps, and compliance visualization against DENR DAO 2016-08. Includes PST monitoring (FAO 235 Series; 60 µg STX-eq/100g).

QGIS Spatial Interpolation DENR DAO 2016-08 PST Monitoring
🐚

Green Mussel Morphometrics

Population-level morphometric analysis of Perna viridis including Kruskal-Wallis & Dunn's post-hoc tests, Spearman correlation, length-weight regression, and PCA biplots — with publication-ready multi-panel R/ggplot2 figures.

Perna viridis PCA Length-Weight R / ggplot2
